The Art of Fragrance

AB started with a simple idea: a good perfume should feel personal. From our small studio in Anthiyur, Erode, we blend rich oils and rare notes to create scents that match your mood and memories.

Every bottle is mixed in small batches, tested for long wear and packed with care, so what reaches you is nothing less than luxury.
